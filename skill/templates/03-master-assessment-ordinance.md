§ **INITIAL ASSESSMENT RESOLUTION.** Each year in which an assessment is first imposed, increased beyond the maximum rate, or changed as described in s. 197.3632(4)(a), the [Governing Body] shall adopt an Initial Assessment Resolution that:
1. describes the service and the Assessed Cost;
1. sets the apportionment method and the proposed rates by Property Use Category;
1. states the **maximum rate** for future years;
1. directs preparation of the preliminary Assessment Roll;
1. sets the date of the public hearing; and
1. directs the mailed and published notices required by Section 6.

§ **NOTICE.** At least 20 days before the hearing, the [Manager] shall:
1. mail notice by first-class U.S. mail to each Owner; and
1. publish notice,

each containing the information required by s. 197.3632(4)(b), Florida Statutes. Failure of an Owner to receive mailed notice does not invalidate the assessment if notice was published and the {City|County} made a good-faith attempt to mail it.

§ **HEARING; FINAL ASSESSMENT RESOLUTION.** At the hearing the [Governing Body] shall receive written objections and hear testimony, may adjust any assessment on the basis of benefit, and may adopt a Final Assessment Resolution that confirms the Assessment Roll. The Final Assessment Resolution is the final adjudication of the issues presented, unless proper steps are taken in a court of competent jurisdiction to secure relief **within [20] days** after the date of the [Governing Body]'s action on it.
