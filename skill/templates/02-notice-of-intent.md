Under the uniform method, a non-ad valorem assessment is collected by the Tax Collector on the same bill as ad valorem taxes. Failure to pay it may result in a tax certificate and possible loss of title. Adopting the resolution does not itself impose an assessment. Any assessment and its rate will be set only after separate mailed and published notice and a public hearing.
