# B. Cost of service study: outline

1. Services in scope and units of service (participants, hours, rentals, permits).
2. Full cost per service: direct personnel, operating, facility (utilities, maintenance, depreciation), and indirect overhead (cost allocation plan).
3. Current revenue and cost recovery % by service.
4. Market comparison (neighboring jurisdictions, private providers).
5. Proposed tier, rate and recovery %. How demand might respond (price elasticity, in plain terms).
6. Projected revenue, net of waivers. The ad valorem need it offsets.
7. Equity review: who pays, and the effect on lower-income and youth users. Mitigation.
