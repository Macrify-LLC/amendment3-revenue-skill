# A. Letter to the county (or other government) about shared-service revenue

Use when part of the loss falls on levies another government administers for the jurisdiction's residents: county EMS, fire MSTUs or fire districts, library cooperatives.

[LETTERHEAD]
[Date]

[County Administrator / County Attorney]
[Address]

Re: Amendment 3 and the funding of [fire / EMS / library] services provided to [City] residents

Dear [Name]:

Amendment 3, approved on November 3, 2026, raises the non-school homestead exemption to $150,000 in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028. Our analysis estimates that revenue from the [County EMS levy / Fire District MSTU / Library Cooperative levy] attributable to property in [City] will fall by about $[AMOUNT] in FY [YYYY] and $[AMOUNT] a year from FY [YYYY] [source / method].

These services are provided under [agreement name and date], which [describe how funding works]. [City] would like to work with the County on how this shortfall will be handled, rather than each of us planning on assumptions about the other. In particular we would like to discuss:

1. The County's estimate of the reduction in each levy, and its plans for the affected services.
2. Whether the County intends to [maintain service levels / adjust funding formulas / consider assessments or fees within the MSTU or MSBU].
3. The timeline. A new non-ad valorem assessment collected on the November [YEAR] tax bill would need a resolution of intent by January 1 (or March 1 by agreement with the Property Appraiser and Tax Collector).
4. [Other items.]

We would welcome a staff meeting by [date]. [Contact.]

Sincerely,
[Name, title]

`[COUNSEL DETERMINATION]` Tone and content, and whether any statements could affect the government's position under existing agreements. Have counsel review before sending.

# B. Terms to raise when renegotiating an interlocal agreement

- **Funding formula.** How the cost of service is shared after the change in taxable value (by calls, units, population, or taxable value).
- **Service levels.** Response-time or staffing commitments, and what happens if funding falls.
- **Who levies what.** Whether the county will impose an MSBU assessment (for fire, not comprehensive EMS) in the service area, or the city will within its limits, with a credit against what the city pays.
- **Transport and medical billing.** Rates and who keeps the revenue.
- **Reopener.** Renegotiation triggered by later legislation implementing Amendment 3 (e.g., under Art. VII, § 6(a)(4) or § 9(a)(2)g).
- **Data sharing.** Incident data and cost allocation, so both sides can support any assessment on the record.
- **Term and exit.** Notice period and transition if either side withdraws.
