# [Service] Assessment Methodology Report: outline

This report is the **record** that supports the legislative findings of special benefit and fair apportionment. The courts defer to the governing body only if the record has competent, substantial evidence (*North Lauderdale*). Get it prepared by, or reviewed with, a qualified rate consultant or the department's own analysts. Every figure needs its data source.

1. **Purpose and scope.** Service, fiscal year, area.
2. **Legal framework (summary).** The two-prong test (*Boca Raton*, *Lake County*, *Sarasota Church of Christ*, *Morris*). Exclusions (*North Lauderdale* for EMS). Note: `[COUNSEL DETERMINATION]` on all legal conclusions.
3. **Service description.** What is provided, where, by whom: stations, apparatus, staffing, response standards; or the collection system; or the stormwater system.
4. **Special benefit analysis.** The logical relationship to property: protection of structures and contents, insurability and premiums (ISO/PPC rating), enabling development and occupancy, containing spread, property value. Cite the data and literature.
5. **Cost base (Assessed Cost).**
   - Starting budget: [department, fund, fiscal year, budget page].
   - Remove: comprehensive EMS and transport [method: e.g., share of incidents by type, unit-hours, or call-type cost allocation]; non-assessable functions [describe]; offsetting revenues (grants, contract revenue, transport billing, inspection fees).
   - Add: allowable indirect costs (cost allocation plan), collection costs (s. 197.3632(8)(c) / PA-TC agreement), statutory discount for early payment (~4%) and uncollectibles.
   - Result: Assessed Cost = $[ ].
6. **Apportionment.**
   - Property Use Categories (map DOR use codes to categories).
   - Demand by category: [incidents by property use, years, data source: NFIRS / CAD].
   - Within category: residential per dwelling unit; non-residential by building square-foot tiers; vacant/agricultural [method]; Tier 1/Tier 2 option (*Morris*).
   - Parcel counts, units and square footage from the tax roll (NAL `DOR_UC`, `NO_RES_UNTS`, `TOT_LVG_AREA`, PA building data).
   - Special rules: RV parks (Ch. 2026-37), mixed-use parcels, multiple buildings, government and exempt property.
7. **Rates.** A rate table by category, at 100% and at partial funding levels (e.g., 50% / 75% / 100% of the Assessed Cost).
8. **Revenue and incidence.**
   - Revenue by category. Exempt-parcel buy-down cost.
   - **Comparison with ad valorem:** for sample parcels (median homestead below/above $250k; condo; small retail; office; warehouse; church), show the ad valorem tax saved under Amendment 3 versus the new assessment.
9. **Findings.** Proposed findings for the ordinance and resolutions.
10. **Appendices.** Data extracts, use-code crosswalk, incident data, cost allocation workpapers.
